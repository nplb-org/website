Operational Improvement Resources

An introduction to the causes of care delays and the overcapacity protocols that keep care moving when demand exceeds supply.

Root Causes of Care Delays

Delays originate at different points on the care pathway. Improvement efforts typically target one of the three domains below.

PDF versions coming soon

Inflow

How patients enter the system, including ED arrivals, transfers, and requests for admission. Managing inflow depends on intake, diversion, and the link between the ED and the community.

Coming soon

Throughput

What occurs while patients are in the ED, including testing, consultation, decision-making, and disposition. Faster throughput reduces time spent in the department.

Coming soon

Outflow

How patients leave the ED, whether to inpatient beds, alternate level of care (ALC), or home. Outflow is often the most binding bottleneck, particularly when ward beds are scarce.

Strategic Overcapacity Protocols

Escalating tiers of action that maintain patient safety and continue care delivery once demand exceeds capacity.

1

Level 1

Early capacity pressure. Routine surge measures and proactive flow activities keep demand and capacity in balance.
