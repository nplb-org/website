Awards & Applications

The purpose of the No Patient Left Behind fund is to provide annual peer-reviewed funding awards to develop and implement promising access-related health system innovations.

Funding Priorities

High-priority proposals fall within several categories, with particular emphasis on those that incorporate patient care accountability or accountability frameworks.

1

Patient Access & System Flow

Improves access to appropriate (right care, right place) care in emergency departments, hospitals, or communities.

2

Accountability Frameworks

Develops or implements patient care accountability concepts or accountability frameworks.

3

Operational Improvement

Process optimization that cuts care delays and improves throughput.

4

Leadership & Capacity Building

Develops effective leaders and trains staff to deliver more efficient patient care.

5

Scalable & Reproducible

Can be repeated and adapted across many healthcare settings and systems.
