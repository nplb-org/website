Leadership: Essential Knowledge

System change begins with leadership. Leaders require a working grasp of accountability, patient flow, and the levers by which a system can be moved.

Effective leaders know their accountability zone and ask how their teams would operate if closing the door were not an option. They hold programs to explicit goals and targets for flow and access. The challenges are systemic rather than a matter of individual provider performance, and the principal levers are operational improvement and accountability frameworks.
